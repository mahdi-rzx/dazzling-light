    Q11.1

    Calculate the amount of heat (in Joules) that is:

    1. achieved when raising the temperature of 5 g. of water from 22°C to 50°C?
    2. required to raise the temperature of 10 g. of water from 10°C to 100°C?

    S11.1

    1. \[ q=m c_{p} \Delta T \]

    \[ q= (5 \cancel{g}) \times \frac{4.184J}{1~ \cancel{gºC}} \times (28 \cancel{ºC}) \]

    \[ q= 586J \]

    1. \[ q=m c_{p} \Delta T \]

    \[ q= (10 \cancel{g}) \times \frac{4.184J}{1~ \cancel{gºC}} \times (90 \cancel{ºC}) \]

    \[ q= 3.77 \times 10^3 J \]

    Q11.1

    Calculate the quantity of heat, in kilojoules, that is:

    1. required to raise the temperature of 12.5 L of water from 24.9 ºC to 35.8 ºC
    2. associated with a 53.3 ºC decrease in temperature in a 9.66 kg aluminum bar (specific heat of aluminum = 0.903 J/gºC)

    S11.1

    1. \[ q~=~m c_{p} \Delta T \]

    \[ m~=~(12.5~\cancel{L}) \times (\dfrac{1,000~\cancel{mL}}{\cancel{L}}) \times (\dfrac{1~\cancel{cm^3}}{1~\cancel{mL}}) \times (\dfrac{1~g}{1~\cancel{cm^3}})~=~12500~g \]

    \[ q~=~(12,500~ \cancel{g}) \times \frac{4.184~J}{1~ \cancel{gºC}} \times (10.9~\cancel{ºC}) \]

    \[ q~=~570070J~=~570.1~kJ \]

    1. \[ q=m c_{p} \Delta T\]

    \[ q~=~(9.66~\cancel{kg.}) \times (\dfrac{1000~\cancel{g.}}{1~\cancel{kg.}}) \times (\dfrac{0.903J}{1~\cancel{gºC}}) \times (-53.3~\cancel{ºC})\]

    \[ q= -464934.834J = -464.9kJ\]

    Q11.1

    Calculate the quantity of heat, in kilojoules, that is:

    1. associated with 4.9 L of water decreasing in temperature from 42.0°C to 33.0°C.
    2. required to raise the temperature of a 3.82 kg lead bar by 51.2°C (specific heat of lead = 0.128 J / g °C).

    S11.1

    a) \[ q = m c_{p} \Delta T \]

    \[ m = (4.9~\cancel{L}) (\dfrac{1,000~\cancel{mL}}{1~\cancel{L}}) (\dfrac{1~\cancel{cm^3}}{1~\cancel{mL}}) (\dfrac{1~g}{1~\cancel{cm^3}}) = 4900 ~\text{g} \]

    \[ q = (4900~\cancel{g}) \frac{4.184~J}{1 \cancel{g°C}} (33.0 – 42.0) \cancel{°C} \]

    \[ q = -184514~\text{J} = -185~\text{kJ} \]

    b) \[ q = m c_{p} \Delta T \]

    \[ q = (3.82~\cancel{kg}) (\dfrac{1000~\cancel{g}}{1~\cancel{kg}}) (\dfrac{0.128~\text{J}}{1~\cancel{g°C}}) (51.2~\cancel{°C}) \]

    \[ q = 25034.752~\text{J} = 25.03~\text{kJ} \]

    Q11.1

    Calculate the final temperature that results from a:

    1. 2.48 g sample of nickel at an initial temperature of 16.7°C that absorbs 59 J of heat (Note: The specific heat capacity of Ni is 0.440 J/g°C)

    2. 72.8 g of water at an initial temperature of 72.5°C that releases 270 J of heat (Note: The specific heat capacity water = 4.184 J/g°C)

    S11.1

    a)

    \[ q=m c_{p} \Delta T\]

    \[ q=m c_{p} (T_f - T_i)\]

    \[ 59J = (2.48g.) \times \frac{0.44~J}{1~g{^{\circ}C}} \times (T_f - 16.7{^{\circ}C}) \]

    \[ T_{f} = \dfrac{59~\cancel{J}}{(2.48~\cancel{g}) \times \frac{0.44~\cancel{J}}{1~\cancel{g}{^{\circ}C}}} + 16.7^{\circ}C \]

    \[ T_f = 70.8{^{\circ}C} \]

    b)

    \[ q=m c_{p} \Delta T\]

    \[ q=m c_{p} (T_f - T_i)\]

    \[ -270J = (72.8g.)\, (4.184 \text J/g{^{\circ}C})\, (T_f - 72.5{^{\circ}C}) \]

    \[ T_{f} = \dfrac{-270~\cancel{J}}{(72.8~\cancel{g}) \times \frac{4.184~\cancel{J}}{1~\cancel{g}{^{\circ}C}}} + 72.5^{\circ}C \]

    \[ T_f = 71.61{^{\circ}C} \]

    Q11.1

    Calculate the quantity of heat, in joules, required in order to;

    1. lower the temperature of 50.0 g water from 25 °C to 15 °C?
    2. raise the temperature of a 4 kg iron bar from 25 °C to 100 °C, given that the specific heat of iron= 0.449 J/g℃

    S11.1

    1. \[ q=m c_{p} \Delta T\]

    \[ q= (50g.) (4.184J/gºC) (-10ºC)\]

    \[ q= -2.092 \times 10^3 J\]

    1. \[ q=m c_{p} \Delta T\]

    \[ q= (4~\cancel{kg.}) (\dfrac{1000g}{1~\cancel{kg}}) (0.449J/gºC) (75ºC)\]

    \[ q= 1.347 \times 10^5 J\]

    Q11.1

    Calculate the quantity of heat, in kilojoules, that is:

    1. needed to raise the temperature of 6.00L of water from 20.0 to 28.5°C;
    2. related to a decrease in temperature from 45.0 °C to 11.0 °C in a 4.47 kg copper bar (specific heat of copper = 0.385 J/g°C).

    S11.1

    1. \[ q = m c_{p} \Delta T\]

    \[ q = (6~\cancel{L}) (\dfrac{1000~\cancel{mL}}{1~\cancel{L}}) (\dfrac{1~g}{1~\cancel{mL}}) (4.184 J/gºC) (8.5ºC) \]

    \[ q = 213384J = 213.4 kJ \]
